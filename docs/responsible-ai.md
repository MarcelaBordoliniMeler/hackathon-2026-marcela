# Responsible AI – FP Agentic Mentor

FP Agentic Mentor includes Responsible AI principles in its design from the start.

| Principle | How it is applied |
|---|---|
| **Human-in-the-loop** | The agent proposes; teachers and learners review and decide. No recommendation replaces the teacher's judgement. |
| **Data minimisation** | Only the information needed to explain a competency or suggest an activity is requested. |
| **Transparency** | Learners always know what was generated by the AI and what was written or edited by a person. |
| **Fairness & inclusion** | Explanations and learning paths adapt to different learning styles, paces and backgrounds. |
| **Reliability & safety** | Suggestions are checked against the official competency framework before they are used. |
| **Respectful voice** | A clear, supportive and non-paternalistic educational tone. |

These principles are aligned with Microsoft's Responsible AI principles (see [`research-notes.md`](research-notes.md)).
