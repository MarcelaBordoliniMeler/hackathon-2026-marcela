# Research notes & references

## Context

- The project is submitted to the **Microsoft Global Hackathon 2026**, in which Microsoft MVPs and Regional Directors join Microsoft employees for a week of collaboration and learning.
- It responds to the Executive Challenge **Hack for Skilling: Turn Skilling into Proven Capability**, focusing on how VET learners can show evidence of real capability.
- The design follows a human–AI collaboration model: the agent acts as a digital worker that supports teachers and learners without replacing human judgement.
