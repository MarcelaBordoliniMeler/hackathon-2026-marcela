## 🧩 Problem

Many VET learners move through their modules without a clear picture of the competencies they must master, how to progress, or how to show what they can actually do. Their evidence of learning ends up scattered across different platforms and folders, and teachers spend a lot of time re-explaining tasks and reviewing work that is not aligned with the competency.

## 💡 Solution

**FP Agentic Mentor** is a conceptual AI agent that works as an *educational digital worker* alongside the teacher. It:

1. **Clarifies competencies** – explains each competency in plain language, with examples, mastery levels and evidence criteria.
2. **Builds learning paths** – proposes a personalised route adapted to the module, the learner's pace and level.
3. **Generates evidence** – suggests practical activities and helps learners assemble a structured, verifiable portfolio.
4. **Coaches progress** – keeps motivation up with feedback, reminders and a visible view of progress.

Every recommendation is reviewed by a teacher or the learner before it is used.

## 🛡️ Responsible AI by design

Human-in-the-loop · data minimisation · transparency about what the AI generated · fairness across learning styles and paces · a clear, respectful educational tone.

More details in [`docs/responsible-ai.md`](docs/responsible-ai.md).
